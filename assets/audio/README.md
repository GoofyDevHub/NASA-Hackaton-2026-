# Selected listening references

Each link plays a 15-second MP3. The stereo 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV alongside it is the approved master.

| Scene | What to listen for | Sound source |
|---|---|---|
| [Amazon lightning](amazon-storm.mp3) | Rain with three thunder groups | Caesar's field recording |
| [Congo lightning](congo-storm.mp3) | Heavier rain with nine thunder groups | The same recording and mapping at higher displayed activity |
| [Antarctic lightning view](antarctica-wind.mp3) | Light wind | Illustrative synthesized airflow. The polar overlay is insufficient to determine lightning activity. |
| [Southern Ocean](antarctica-ocean-deep-male.mp3) | A low male singing voice in three phrases | VocalSet singer m3 |
| [Atlantic near the Amazon mouth](amazon-ocean-soprano.mp3) | A soprano in four phrases | VocalSet singer f1 |
| [Congo aerosols](congo-air-pollution-cough-dense.mp3) | Thirteen groups of coughing with short pauses | Nine excerpts from two recordings by ezwa |

Caesar recorded rain and thunder in Sundbyberg, Sweden, and released the recording into the public domain. ORBIT filters and layers excerpts according to the NASA lightning map. [Rain and thunder](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_and_thunder.ogg)

The singers come from *VocalSet* by Julia Wilkins, Prem Seetharaman, Alison Wahl and Bryan Pardo (2018). ORBIT arranges recorded vowels, adjusts phrase duration and level, applies small pitch shifts to soprano phrases and adds a room tail to the low male voice. [Dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/1203819) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

ezwa's public-domain recordings supply the coughs, excerpted, faded and scheduled at their original pitch and speed. Smoke display coverage drives the sound. The track conveys an artistic association with polluted air. [Cough 1](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cough_1.ogg) · [Cough 2](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cough_2.ogg)
