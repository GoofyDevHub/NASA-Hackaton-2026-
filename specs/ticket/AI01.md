# AI01 · Answer a question about the selected image

**Area** Obit

**Depends on** [CAT02](CAT02.md)

Choose the demo's conversation service and wire a text question to the selected scene, source and mapping. Keep provider credentials outside client files.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Obit answers what the sound means and identifies its source and period in plain language.
- [ ] Ask for today's polar lightning rate or Congo AQI. The answer explains why the scene cannot supply that value. Service failure leaves the player usable.

## Verify

Review answers for one ocean, one storm and the Congo aerosol track against their source notes. Switch regions between two questions.
