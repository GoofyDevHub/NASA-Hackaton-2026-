# AI03 · Have a hands-free conversation after activation

**Area** Obit

**Depends on** [AI02](AI02.md)

Connect Talk to Obit to microphone input and spoken replies in the selected demo browser. Retain the typed interaction.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Activation names the microphone choice. Listening state, cancellation and transcript are accessible. Ending the interaction releases capture.
- [ ] The assistant never acts on its own spoken reply. A denied permission, lost connection or unavailable speech service exposes the typed or manual path.

## Verify

Test with a microphone and with permission denied. Say “Play the Southern Ocean”, then “Stop”. Check the actual track and listen for overlapping speech.

> **Paused, 15 November 2026.** Obit was removed from the build. Voice control (`site/voice.js`, see `site/README.md`) covers hands-free commands in English and Italian but does not answer questions about an image, so this ticket stays open until the team decides whether Obit returns.
