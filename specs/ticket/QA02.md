# QA02 · Check that the sound differences are understandable

**Area** Validation

**Depends on** [WEB03](WEB03.md), [CAT02](CAT02.md)

Listen at a comfortable common volume on the judging speakers and headphones. Play Amazon and Congo in shuffled order and ask which carries denser activity.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Log the answers and listening setup. Verify audible wind, relative storm density, vocal contrast and cough-only gaps.
- [ ] Disclose what the samples encode and record any confusion. Capture feedback separately from file-integrity checks.

## Verify

Replay the pair at unchanged volume, then ask a listener what the polar wind and Congo coughs do and do not establish. Record observations and untested conditions.
