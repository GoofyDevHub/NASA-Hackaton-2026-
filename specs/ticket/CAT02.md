# CAT02 · Write scene descriptions and listening notes

**Area** Media

**Depends on** [CAT01](CAT01.md)

Prepare concise English description, sound-key text and source citation per view for the player and assistant.

## Acceptance

- [ ] All nine notes identify location, phenomenon and source period. Playable notes explain an audible cue. Image-only notes explain availability.
- [ ] The wind note states insufficient polar coverage. The low voice identifies its broader region. Coughs are described as an artistic response to smoke colours.

## Verify

Review each note beside the actual image, legend and measurements. Check that none gives live weather, inferred illness, physical temperatures or a historical trend.
